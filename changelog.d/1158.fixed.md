- `VertexLayout` no longer documents the wrong vertex strides. `.position`,
  `.positionColor` and `.positionNormalColor` were described as 12, 28 and 40
  bytes, but `makeDescriptor()` has always emitted 16, 32 and 48 — the smaller
  numbers count `float3` as 12 packed bytes, while the descriptor lays it out in
  the 16-byte slot a `SIMD3<Float>` occupies. Only the documentation was wrong;
  the descriptors are unchanged, so no buffer you already fill needs resizing
  ([#1158](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1158))
