- The GLSL shaders that shipped beside the Processing example ports are gone —
  all 28 `Examples/**/data/*.glsl`, plus `Examples/Topics/Shaders/Landscape/Landscape.pde`
  ([#1190](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1190)). Eight of them declared
  GPLv2, LGPL-2.1 or CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 in their own headers, which this repository's MIT
  license cannot absorb — CC **BY-NC** is non-commercial only and collides head-on with
  MIT's grant of "the rights to ... sell copies", and the GPLv2 headers pointed at a
  `COPYING` file that was never shipped. The remaining 20 declared no license but shared
  the same provenance, so they went together rather than leaving a category behind with
  no way to tell the safe files from the rest.
- Nothing to do when upgrading: no target declared them as resources and nothing read
  them at runtime, so no example's output changes. If you were reading them as reference
  material for a Metal translation, fetch the GLSL from
  [processing/processing-examples](https://github.com/processing/processing-examples)
  instead — each sketch's `.pde` names what it loaded.
- `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` no longer claims that metaphor redistributes third-party
  material "in one place: three fonts". It now lists all four categories — fonts,
  Processing example originals, example media, and public-domain texts — states what
  `LICENSE` (MIT) actually covers, and records that the credited Processing originals
  carry no license grant at all
  ([#1191](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1191)).
