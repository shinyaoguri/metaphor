- Setting `OrbitCamera.damping` back to `0` now throws away the glide that was
  in flight, instead of freezing it. `update()` returns early while damping is
  off, so the stored speed never decayed: the camera stood still for as long as
  damping stayed at `0` and then took off on its own the moment the value was
  raised again, replaying the whole glide — measured at 0.55 rad (32°) over a
  full second after a short drag, and identical whether the wait was 10 frames
  or 10 seconds. It was easy to hit wherever damping is exposed as a control, a
  GUI slider or a parameter store among them. Raising damping again now leaves
  the camera where it is; a new drag glides as before
  ([#1128](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1128)).
