- `orbitControl()` no longer spins the camera on the frame a mouse button goes
  down. It looked only at "is a button down" and "how far did the cursor move
  since last frame", and a press is both of those at once: `handleMouseDown`
  advances `mouseX`/`mouseY` to the press point while `pmouseX`/`pmouseY` stay
  a frame behind, so the whole jump to the press point was applied as a drag.
  A single click at the centre of a 1280×800 canvas, with no movement at all,
  turned the camera by 3.2 rad (about 183°) and pinned the elevation at its
  lower limit. It was easy to hit whenever the cursor arrives somewhere without
  a `mouseMoved` in between: clicking without ever crossing the canvas first,
  injected input (`METAPHOR_VIEWER=1` stdin, the MCP `input` tool), and the
  live viewer forwarding a press at an off-canvas coordinate. The press frame
  is now measured from the press point instead of from `pmouse`, so a press on
  its own rotates by zero while a drag that lands in the same frame still
  rotates by the drag distance. `pmouseX`/`pmouseY` keep their meaning — the
  cursor position one frame ago — and sketches reading them directly are
  unaffected ([#1100](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1100))
