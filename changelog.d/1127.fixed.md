- `OrbitCamera` no longer keeps pressing against the elevation limits after a
  drag runs into them. `update()` clamped the angle to `minElevation` /
  `maxElevation` but left the velocity that pushed it there alive, so with
  `damping` on, a drag that swung all the way to the top pinned the camera
  against the limit for the rest of the glide — with `damping = 0.9`, 350–550 ms
  after letting go, during which a drag back down was eaten by the leftover
  velocity before it moved the camera at all (7–15 frames at 60fps). A frame
  whose elevation gets clamped now drops the elevation velocity too, so the
  camera stops at the limit and reverses on the very next frame. Azimuth has no
  limits and its glide is untouched, including on the frames elevation is
  clamped ([#1127](https://github.com/shinyaoguri/metaphor/issues/1127)).
